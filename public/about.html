Global Monument Atlas

Tracking the public budgets, visitor records, and documentation behind government recognized architectural monuments worldwide.

117,533 sites 195 countries & 37 territories 322 public APIs & registers 209 published budgets

Methodology

This atlas brings together public records for government recognized architectural monuments worldwide. National monument lists are cross-checked against public encyclopedias, international registries, and official government budget documents to show how monuments are funded and maintained. Natural parks, peaks, and landforms are removed so the atlas focuses only on architectural monuments, and undisclosed budgets or visitor figures are always left unreported rather than estimated.
